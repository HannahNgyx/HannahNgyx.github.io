# SPEC — the site as it is

Each rule has an ID, a layer tag, a statement and the widths it is checked at:
`- **HERO-1** [base] The page … — All`

- **Widths:** **1440, 1024, 768, 390** (`design/site.json`). "All" means all four.
- **Layers:** `[base]` rules must hold with enhance off (`?enhance=off`) and with it on. `[enhance]` rules describe what enhance adds, and are checked with enhance on.
- **Breakpoints:** **narrow** is `@media (max-width: 30rem)` (480px, `--bp-narrow`): 390 is narrow, 768 and up are not. **stack** is `@media (max-width: 64rem)` (1024px, `--bp-stack`): 1024, 768 and 390 are at or below it (no video cards, VIDEO-4), 1440 is not. HERO's “desktop” also needs a hover-capable pointer (see the HERO intro). Record new breakpoints here when a ticket adds one.

Rules describe what exists. To change the site, a ticket changes the rule text first (designer), then the code. IDs are never reused: removed rules are struck through (`~~HERO-4~~ removed in #12`), and new rules take the next free number.

**Sections.** Each page section, and each shared part, has a heading naming its folder under `src/` (house rule S-4). The heading's prefix is the prefix of its rule IDs. A ticket that adds a section adds its heading and folder together. VIDEO is part of the hero's folder.

**Restructure (October 2026).** Rules that mixed both layers were split. The larger part kept the ID and the smaller part took the next free number: the motion and rainbow of NAV-6, NAV-7, NAV-8 and HERO-7 moved to NAV-12, NAV-11, NAV-13 and HERO-11; the enhance-off behaviour of HERO-9 and VIDEO-5 became HERO-12 and VIDEO-8. With enhance off, the rainbow shows as a solid colour (house rule L-6).

- **GLOBAL-2** [base] The document is English (`<html lang="en">`), titled "Hannah Nguyen", with a viewport meta tag. — All
- **GLOBAL-3** [base] No horizontal scroll and no element extends past the viewport. — All
- **GLOBAL-4** [base] There is exactly one h1. — All
- **GLOBAL-5** [base] Every focusable element shows a 2px solid `--color-text` outline, offset 3px, on `:focus-visible`, except where an `[enhance]` rule says otherwise (the controls on the rainbow pill, NAV-10). — All
- **GLOBAL-6** [base] A `<header>` sits above `<main id="main">` and holds the site nav (NAV). — All

## NAV — shared/nav
“Narrow” in these rules means `@media (max-width: 30rem)`: 390 is narrow, 768 and up are not. “Desktop” means the same as in HERO: wider than 30rem with a hover-capable pointer (`(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`); 1440, 1024 and 768 are checked as desktop. The rainbow is `--gradient-rainbow` (six stops, `--rainbow-red` … `--rainbow-violet`, each at least 4.5:1 on `--color-bg`, on `--color-surface` and under `--color-on-ink`); it never moves, and it appears only with enhance on.

- **NAV-1** [base] The header holds `<nav aria-label="Main">` drawn as a pill: background `--color-surface` (with enhance on, the rainbow on screens without desktop hover, NAV-10), corners `--radius-pill`, width `min(60rem, 100% - 2 × --space-3)`, centred, `--space-5` below the top of the page. — All
- **NAV-2** [base] Inside the pill, a link “Hannah Nguyen” (weight 600, href `#main`) sits at the inline-start. The links “Projects”, “Experience”, “Endorsements”, “Contact” (in that order, hrefs `#projects`, `#experience`, `#endorsements`, `#contact`) sit at the inline-end. All are `--color-text` at `--fs-body` (`--color-on-ink` on the rainbow pill, NAV-10); “Contact” has the same colour and weight as the others. All sit on one row at 1440, 1024 and 768. When narrow, the four section links sit in the menu instead (NAV-4, NAV-6). — All
- **NAV-3** [base] Every nav link is at least 44×44px. — All
- **NAV-4** [base] When narrow, the pill holds one row: the “Hannah Nguyen” link at the inline-start and a `<button type="button">` reading “Menu” (accessible name “Menu”) at the inline-end, at least 44×44px, with `aria-controls` pointing at the menu and `aria-expanded` saying whether the menu is open. The four section links are not shown in the pill. At 768 and up there is no Menu button. — All
- **NAV-5** [base] Nav links are not underlined at rest and get an underline on `:hover`. Focus follows GLOBAL-5. — All
- **NAV-6** [base] When narrow, pressing Menu opens the menu: a panel in `--color-surface` with `--radius-lg` corners, `--space-2` below the pill and aligned to its inline-end edge, holding the four section links in the NAV-2 order, one per row, each at least 44px tall. It lies over the page and moves nothing. It closes when Menu is pressed again, on Escape, on choosing one of its links, and on a click outside the nav; when it closes, focus returns to the Menu button. Closed, the menu is invisible, cannot be clicked, cannot be reached with Tab and is hidden from assistive tech (`visibility: hidden`). With enhance off it opens and closes at once; NAV-12 adds the fade. — 390
- **NAV-7** [base] In the pill, a decorative divider sits on “Contact”’s inline-start side, centred in the gap between “Endorsements” and “Contact” and vertically centred on the link text: `--border-w` (2px) wide, `--cap-body` × 1em (about 12px, the body font’s cap height) tall, solid `--color-text` (NAV-11 fills it with the rainbow). It is a pseudo-element: it takes no space, cannot be clicked and is not read out. — 1440, 1024, 768
- **NAV-8** [base] In the narrow menu (NAV-6) there is no vertical divider; instead a horizontal line `--border-w` (2px) thick, solid `--color-text` (NAV-13 makes it the rainbow), runs along the top edge of the “Contact” row, the full width of the row. The menu panel stays `--color-surface` with `--color-text` links. — 390
- **NAV-9** [enhance] On desktop the pill stays `--color-surface`, and a hard-edged circular spot of rainbow can show on it. The spot is drawn by two `aria-hidden`, non-interactive layers inside the nav, both clipped to the pill’s shape and masked by the same circle (centre `--spot-x` `--spot-y`, measured in px from the nav’s top-left corner; radius `--spot-r`): `.nav__spot`, the rainbow across the pill’s whole width (so the spot’s colour is whatever stop lies under it), painted over the nav’s text; and `.nav__ghost`, a copy of the nav’s text row in `--color-on-ink`, painted over that. Inside the circle, the background is the rainbow and any text is `--color-on-ink` (at least 6.4:1 on every stop); outside it, nothing changes. Without `data-spot` on the nav, `--spot-r` is 0 and no spot shows (the state at load and in screenshots). With `data-spot`, `--spot-r` is `--nav-spot-r` (1.5rem, a 48px circle). A hovered link keeps its underline inside the spot. The whole pill never changes colour at once. The spot follows a mouse pointer only: listeners are on the nav (passive pointer events, not the window) and exist only while `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine) and (width > 30rem)` matches; touch and pen pointers are ignored, and with no listeners the nav never gets `data-spot`. While the mouse is over the nav, `--spot-x` / `--spot-y` equal the pointer’s `clientX − nav left` / `clientY − nav top` in px, updated at most once per animation frame with no transition or easing on position, so the circle stays centred on the pointer. Entering the nav sets the position first, then adds `data-spot`; leaving removes it. `--spot-r` (a registered `<length>`) grows 0 → `--nav-spot-r` on enter and shrinks back to 0 on leave over `--dur-quick` (200ms) with `--ease-state` (`ease`), staying where the pointer left; this transition of a registered custom property is an exception to M-1 (a mask circle can't be scaled without scaling the text under it). Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the spot still follows the pointer but appears and disappears instantly (no transition on `--spot-r`). Nothing about the spot runs on its own. With enhance off, neither layer is rendered. — 1440, 1024, 768
- **NAV-10** [enhance] When not desktop (narrow, or no hover-capable pointer), the pill’s background is the rainbow running left to right (`--gradient-rainbow`), and every control on it (the “Hannah Nguyen” link, the section links when they sit in the pill, and the Menu button) is `--color-on-ink` (at least 6.4:1 on every stop), with a `--color-on-ink` focus outline (GLOBAL-5). The Contact divider (NAV-7) is `--color-on-ink` on it. There is no cursor spot (NAV-9). The narrow menu panel is not part of the pill and keeps NAV-8’s colours. — 390
- **NAV-11** [enhance] On desktop the Contact divider (NAV-7) is filled with the rainbow running top to bottom (`--gradient-rainbow-vertical`). — 1440, 1024, 768
- **NAV-12** [enhance] Opening the narrow menu (NAV-6) fades it in (opacity 0 → 1) while it moves 4px (`--space-1`) down into place, over `--dur-quick` (200ms) with `--ease-state` (`ease`); closing runs the same in reverse, and it is hidden and unclickable once the fade ends. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` it opens and closes instantly. — 390
- **NAV-13** [enhance] The narrow menu's Contact line (NAV-8) is the rainbow, left to right (`--gradient-rainbow`). — 390

## HERO — pages/home/hero
“Desktop” in these rules means a viewport wider than 30rem whose pointer can hover (`(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`); 1440, 1024 and 768 are checked as desktop. 390, and any screen without hover, are not desktop. The words come from `content.json` in the hero's folder.

- **HERO-1** [base] `<main>` starts with a `<section>` labelled by its h1. The h1’s text is one text node reading exactly “Hey, I’m Hannah” (its accessible name is the same, read once), in `--font-display` (Fraunces 400, optical size automatic, from Google Fonts with a serif fallback; only the h1 uses it) at `--fs-display`, colour `--color-text`. On desktop it sits on one line with its first letter set large (HERO-8); at 390 it takes no more than two lines. — All
- **HERO-2** [base] Below the h1, a `<p>` reads “Full-stack software engineer with experience shipping alongside stakeholders” at `--fs-lead`, max 28ch wide, text-align end, in three lines at 1440, 1024 and 768. Its inline-end edge is within 2px of the inline-end edge of the h1’s text at 1440, 1024 and 768. At 390 it is centred under the h1. — All
- **HERO-3** [base] On desktop, a figure box carrying the hook `data-photo-reveal` stands at the inline-start of the big H (HERO-8). It has a 3:4 aspect ratio and is `--figure-h` tall: 1.6 × the H’s cap height, within 2px, so the H comes up to her shoulder. Its bottom edge is within 2px of the H’s baseline. It overlaps the H so that her back (x 460 of the 600-wide box) is 0–12px past the H’s left ink edge: she leans on the H. At rest the box shows a dotted drawing of Hannah: an inline SVG path (under 3 KB) traced from the hero's `assets/hannah-cutout.png` in the same 600×800 viewBox. The path is her outer silhouette plus the edges of her face, arm and hand, so her head, bun, shoulder, bent arm and the card she holds can be told apart. It is drawn in round dots `--outline-dot` wide in `--color-text`, spaced `--outline-gap` apart. On her lower torso, below the elbow, it shows the words “Hover to see me” on two lines (“Hover to” / “see me”) in the body font at `--fs-hint`, `--color-text`. No glyph comes within 2px of a dot at 1440, 1024 or 768. The outline and the words are `aria-hidden`. In the same box, exactly over the outline, sits the colour photo of Hannah cut out onto a transparent background (`assets/hannah-cutout.png`, head to hip, no shop behind her, no grayscale filter), with width/height attributes and alt text “Hannah Nguyen”. At rest the photo is hidden (opacity 0). At 390, or on any screen without hover, the figure is not rendered (`display: none`). — All
- **HERO-4** [base] Below the subtitle, a row holds two links of the same height (at least 3rem / 48px) with the same `--radius-sm` corners and a `--border-w` (2px) `--color-text` border, `--space-4` apart, centred under the hero group (HERO-6): “My resume” (fill `--color-text`, text `--color-on-ink`, href = the bundled `Hannah_Nguyen_resume_2026.pdf`) and “Contact me” (transparent fill, text `--color-text`, href `#contact`). They sit on one row at every width. — All
- **HERO-5** [base] Below the buttons, a `<p>` reads “I worked at: Tarmac.IO, Azimuthsemi.com, Zyro, MiTek”. Each company name is a link: “Tarmac.IO” to https://tarmac.io, “Azimuthsemi.com” to https://azimuthsemi.com, “Zyro” to https://thezyro.com/ and “MiTek” to https://www.mitek-us.com. At rest all four are `--color-text` and underlined in `--color-text` (hover and focus: HERO-9, HERO-12), and each link’s hit area is at least 44×44px, with no two hit areas overlapping. At 1440, 1024 and 768 it is one line. At 390, “I worked at:” sits on its own line and the four links share the line below it, with a line-height of 1.5 (the 44px hit areas come from each link’s `::after` layer, not from the line spacing). — All
- **HERO-6** [base] The hero group is centred horizontally in the viewport, within 1px, starting `--space-7` below the top of `<main>`. On desktop the group is the figure, the big H and the text column; elsewhere it is the text column alone, with the h1 and subtitle centred at 390. — All
- **HERO-7** [base] Hovering the figure reveals the colour photo and hides the outline and the hint. The hover target is the whole figure box (the big H does not block it). The switch is opacity only, and nothing on the page moves or resizes. With enhance off it switches at once; HERO-11 adds the cross-fade. — 1440, 1024, 768
- **HERO-8** [base] On desktop, the h1’s first letter is the big H. It is styled through `::first-letter`, so the h1’s text is unchanged. It is set in Fraunces 400 at `--fs-initial` and stands at the inline-start of both the rest of the h1 (“ey, I’m Hannah”, which follows it directly on the first line) and the subtitle. The H’s cap top is within 2px of the cap top of the h1’s other capitals, and its baseline is within 2px of the subtitle’s last baseline. At 390, and on any screen without hover, there is no big H: the h1 is set at one size. — All
- **HERO-9** [enhance] On `:hover` and on `:focus-visible`, each worked-at link’s text shows the rainbow (`--gradient-rainbow`, left to right across the name, through `background-clip: text`) and keeps its underline; at rest it is `--color-text`. The rainbow is an `aria-hidden` copy of the name (`.hero__company-rainbow`) laid exactly over the name (`.hero__company-name`): at rest the copy is opacity 0 and the name opacity 1; on hover or focus the copy is opacity 1 and the name opacity 0, so nothing moves or resizes. Screen readers hear each company name once. All of it sits inside `@supports (background-clip: text) or (-webkit-background-clip: text)`; without that support the link behaves as HERO-12. The rainbow does not move. Switching between rest and hover/focus (both ways) is an opacity-only cross-fade of the name and its copy over `--dur-quick` (200ms) with `--ease-state` (`ease`); nothing else animates and nothing runs on its own. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the switch is instant (no transition). — All
- **HERO-10** [base] The worked-at line (HERO-5) is set at `--fs-small` (0.875rem, 14px), smaller than the subtitle (`--fs-lead`, 18–22px) and the buttons. — All
- **HERO-11** [enhance] The figure's reveal (HERO-7) is a cross-fade of `--dur-quick` (200ms) with `--ease-state` (`ease`), opacity only. With reduced motion the switch is instant (no transition). — 1440, 1024, 768
- **HERO-12** [base] With enhance off, each worked-at link stays `--color-text` and underlined on hover and focus; its rainbow copy is in the markup but not displayed (`display: none`), so the name is read once. — All

## VIDEO — pages/home/hero
Project video cards in the hero section. Their content (title, video file, link) comes from `videos.projects` in the hero's `content.json`, four entries of `{ title, video, href }` in order 1–4. Video files live in `public/videos/project<n>.mp4` (served as-is, not bundled), encoded with the command in `public/videos/README.md`. The cards exist only above 64rem; at `@media (max-width: 64rem)` (`--bp-stack`) they are not rendered (VIDEO-4), so the rules below are checked at 1440.

- **VIDEO-1** [base] The hero section contains `<ul aria-label="Project videos">` with four `<li>` in DOM order 1–4, after the hero group. Each `<li>` holds one `<a>` whose href is its entry’s `href` (placeholders: `#projects`), whose accessible name is its entry’s title (placeholders: “Project 1” … “Project 4”), and which has no click handler. The `<a>` is the card: a 16:9 box with a `--border-w` `--color-text` border, `--color-bg` fill, square corners and no shadow, at least 44×44px, with the GLOBAL-5 focus ring. When the entry’s `video` is null the card shows the centred placeholder text “Video #N” over “Project N” (the title, weight 600), both `--fs-body` `--color-text`; “Video #N” is `aria-hidden`. When `video` is set, the card holds a `<video>` filling the box (`object-fit: cover`, `aria-hidden`) with `muted`, `loop`, `playsinline`, `preload="metadata"`, no `controls`, and the title stays in the card as visually hidden text. — 1440
- **VIDEO-2** [base] Above 64rem: cards 1 (upper) and 2 (lower) sit to the inline-start of the hero group, and cards 3 (upper) and 4 (lower) to its inline-end, in two equal side columns that keep the group centred (HERO-6). Each card is `--card-w` wide, or narrower when its side column is too narrow, so no card or rotated corner crosses `<main>`’s side padding. The upper cards are `--space-5` clear of the group (the figure on desktop, otherwise the text column); the lower cards sit `--space-5` closer to the centre, against the group’s edge beside the buttons row, and so stay at least `--space-5` clear of the buttons and the worked-at line. The upper cards’ tops are level with the h1’s top (±`--space-5`) and the lower cards’ tops are level with the buttons row’s top (±`--space-5`). The cards do not overlap the nav, the figure, the big H, the h1, the subtitle, the buttons or the worked-at line. These clearances hold at rest; VIDEO-6 keeps them while floating, and a hovered or focused card follows VIDEO-7 instead. — 1440
- **VIDEO-3** [base] Each card has a static 2D tilt set with the CSS `rotate` property (not `transform`): card 1 −3deg, card 2 4deg, card 3 −2deg, card 4 3deg. Each card (the `<a>`) carries `data-float` (the float and expand hook, VIDEO-6 and VIDEO-7). The cards stack above any hero-level decoration (the list is `z-index: 2`, and each card has an opaque `--color-bg` fill). — 1440
- **VIDEO-4** [base] At 64rem and below (`@media (max-width: 64rem)`, `--bp-stack`) the cards’ list is not rendered (`display: none`), so the cards are not shown, cannot be reached with Tab and are not in the accessibility tree. The hero there is unchanged from before the cards existed: no gap or space is left where they would be, and the hero group stays centred (HERO-6). — 1024, 768, 390
- **VIDEO-5** [enhance] Video playback: a card’s `<video>` carries `data-autoplay`, has no `autoplay` attribute and no `controls`, and its `src` ends in `#t=0.1`. Without reduced motion, it plays (muted, looping) while any part of it is in the viewport and is paused while it is fully off screen. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` it never starts: it stays paused at 0.1s with that frame painted in the card (not a blank or black box). If the preference changes while the page is open, a playing video pauses on its current frame when reduce turns on, and an on-screen video resumes playing when it turns off. Playback causes no console errors (a refused `play()` is caught silently). — 1440
- **VIDEO-6** [enhance] Each `[data-float]` card drifts up and down between 0 and −`--float-y` (6px) on a `--dur-float` (6s) `ease-in-out` loop (`alternate infinite`), animated with the `translate` property only (never `transform` or `rotate`, so VIDEO-3’s tilt is kept). The four cards start at different points in the loop (cards 1–4: delays 0, −1.5s, −3s, −4.5s), so they never move in sync. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the cards don’t animate (`animation: none`) and sit exactly at their VIDEO-2 positions. At every point of the float, each card keeps VIDEO-2’s `--space-5` clearance and stays inside `<main>`’s side padding. — 1440
- **VIDEO-7** [enhance] On devices with hover (`(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`), hovering a card scales it to `--scale-hover` (1.06) over `--dur-quick` (200ms) `--ease-state` (`ease`), using the `scale` property only, and it returns the same way on leave. `:focus-visible` on a card gives the same scale on any device, and the GLOBAL-5 focus ring still shows, tilted and scaled with the card. The tilt (`rotate`) is unchanged. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the scale change is instant (no transition). While a card is scaled (at any point of its float), it is exempt from VIDEO-2’s `--space-5` clearance, but it still does not overlap the nav, the figure or the hero text, and it stays inside `<main>`’s side padding. — 1440
- **VIDEO-8** [base] With enhance off, the videos never start: each stays paused at 0.1s with that frame painted in the card (not a blank or black box), and the cards neither float nor scale. — 1440
